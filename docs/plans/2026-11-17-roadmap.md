# Engineering standards roadmap

Detailed planning begins when a milestone starts. Follow its GitHub milestone for the published scope and issues, and the [Engineering Standards board](https://github.com/users/edward-sf/projects/11) for current status.

| Milestone | Outcome | Plan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [M0 · Engineering Foundation](https://github.com/edward-sf/standards/milestone/1) | A new project can adopt a documented development and review workflow. | [M0 tasks](2026-11-17-m0-engineering-foundation.md) |
| [M1 · Quality, Security & Operational Standards](https://github.com/edward-sf/standards/milestone/2) | Projects can assess testing, security, documentation, and delivery readiness. | [M1 tasks](2026-11-19-m1-quality-security-operations.md) |
| [M2 · AI Engineering Standards](https://github.com/edward-sf/standards/milestone/3) | Teams can design and evaluate reliable AI applications and agent workflows. | Planned when M2 starts |
| [M3 · Automation & Standards Enforcement](https://github.com/edward-sf/standards/milestone/4) | Client repos can adopt reusable checks, templates, and shared configurations. | Planned when M3 starts |
| [M4 · Governance, Versioning & Continuous Improvement](https://github.com/edward-sf/standards/milestone/5) | Standards can evolve with documented releases, migration, and adoption tracking. | Planned when M4 starts |
